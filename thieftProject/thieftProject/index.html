ABOUT THE SYSTEM

A Secure Digital Platform for Theft Reporting and Tracking

The Nationwide Software-Based Theft Reporting and Tracking System is a centralized web platform designed to simplify theft reporting and case tracking across the country. The system enables citizens, businesses, police officers and government authorities to work together through one secure platform while improving transparency, accountability and service delivery.

Vision

To provide a trusted nationwide digital platform that enhances public safety through efficient theft reporting and transparent case tracking.

Mission

To improve collaboration between citizens and law enforcement agencies by providing a secure, accessible and reliable reporting system.

Project Objectives

Online Reporting

Develop a web application for reporting theft incidents from anywhere in the country.

Secure Records

Store theft records securely to support investigations and legal processes.

Analytics

Generate reports and dashboards to support government planning and decision making.

Public Safety

Support nationwide efforts in preventing and managing theft incidents.

Who Can Use This System?

Citizen

Police Officer

Review reports, investigate cases, update investigation status and communicate with complainants.

Administrator

Manage users, reports, system configuration and security.

Policy Maker
